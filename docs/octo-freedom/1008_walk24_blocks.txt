WHY THE INCOMING LEG IS ON THE SAME TIMELINE. The chain is always in the market, so a leg's OPEN
BAR *is* the previous leg's EXIT BAR. Relocating an open therefore relocates the previous leg's
exit. A leg that follows a STOP is the exception: there the incoming leg closed at its breach bar
and the re-entry router chose the open, so the block carries three incoming timestamps — the
breach, the ws1x return bar, and the conf bar that became this leg's open.

`pct for THIS leg` is measured from THIS leg's own open on THIS leg's own side, so the incoming
leg's rows read NEGATIVE where price was beyond this open. That gap is exactly what a relocated
open gives up or gains on the incoming side.

Rows marked `incoming:` are the previous leg's walk events. Rows with no prefix are this leg's.
